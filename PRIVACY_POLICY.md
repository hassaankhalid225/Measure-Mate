# Privacy Policy — Measure Mate

**Last updated: 17 August 2026**

Measure Mate ("the app") is a unit converter and calculator published by GTS.
This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Short version

Measure Mate does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no
internet access at all.

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

The app does not collect, store on a server, or transmit any personal or
sensitive user data. Specifically, it does **not** collect:

- names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account information
- location data
- contacts, photos, files, or messages
- device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
- health or fitness data (values you type into the BMI calculator are used only
  to display a result on screen and are never stored or sent anywhere)

## Internet access

The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission on Android. It is technically
incapable of sending data anywhere. All conversions and calculations run
entirely on your device, and the app works with no network connection.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves a small set of your own preferences locally, using the operating
system's standard app-storage:

- your selected theme (light, dark, or system)
- which converters and calculators you have added to your home screen
- the units you last used in each converter

This information never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is
not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently. You can also
clear it at any time through your device's *Settings → Apps → Measure Mate →
Storage → Clear data*.

## Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions. It does not access the camera,
microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other protected resource.

## Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, and
no third-party tracking SDKs. No data is shared with anyone because none is
collected.

## Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.
Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address
and the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Any future version that
introduces data collection will be accompanied by an app update and, where
required, a request for your consent.
